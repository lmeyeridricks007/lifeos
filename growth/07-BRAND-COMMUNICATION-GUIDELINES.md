# 07 — Brand & Communication Guidelines

ExpatCopilot should sound like a **sharp, calm friend who has done the research**—not a government desk, not a hype startup, not a link farm.

---

## Global tone

**Be:** useful · clear · practical · informed · friendly · trustworthy · data-aware · expat-focused · non-salesy  

**Avoid:**
- Spammy backlink requests  
- Fake urgency (“do this TODAY or fail”)  
- Empty “ultimate guide” clichés  
- Overclaiming (“guaranteed visa”, “best bank always”)  
- Obvious generic AI voice (no substance, no specifics)  
- Dropping links without answering the question  
- Motivational fluff  
- Implying official government authority  

**Always:** encourage verification on official sources (IND, Belastingdienst, gemeente, etc.) when stakes are legal/financial.

---

## Channel-specific tone

### Website
- Structured, scannable, precise  
- Disclaimers where needed; methodology when citing numbers  
- CTAs invite action (“check”, “compare”, “plan”) not hype  

### Reddit
- Answer first; be specific to the OP’s situation  
- Match sub norms; lurk before posting  
- Links only when they add a tool/checklist the reply can’t hold  
- No brand evangelism threads  

### Facebook groups
- Neighborly and practical  
- Respect group rules (many ban links—provide value in-text)  
- Short paragraphs; concrete steps  
- Disclose affiliation if asked  

### LinkedIn
- Professional, insight-led  
- Founder voice > corporate brochure  
- Good for HR/recruiter angles and policy/salary context  
- One clear takeaway + optional link  

### Instagram
- Visual clarity over jargon  
- Carousels: one idea per slide  
- Captions can be warmer; still factual  
- Avoid aesthetic-only posts with no utility  

### Email
- Respect inbox time  
- Subject = utility promise  
- One primary CTA  
- Personal but not chatty-chaos  

### Journalist outreach
- Newsworthy angle in first 2 sentences  
- Offer data, chart, or expert clarification  
- Easy cite (URL, methodology, contact)  
- No attachments walls; no buzzword salad  

### Partner outreach
- Mutual audience benefit  
- Clear what you offer (resource kit, co-checklist)  
- Clear ask (link, embed, employee send)  
- No hard sell on affiliates in first touch  

---

## Phrasing swaps

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| “Ultimate guide to…” | “Practical checklist for…” |
| “You must act now” | “Before you sign / register, verify…” |
| “We are the #1…” | “Here’s a clear way to compare…” |
| “Guaranteed approval” | “Common requirements—confirm on official sites” |
| “Smash that link” | “If useful, the checker is here:” |

---

## Compliance & trust notes

- Not legal, tax, or immigration advice  
- Provider mentions ≠ endorsement  
- Affiliate disclosure when relevant  
- Never fabricate statistics
