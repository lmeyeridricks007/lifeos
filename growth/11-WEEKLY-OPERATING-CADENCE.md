# 11 — Weekly Operating Cadence

Sustainable rhythm for ExpatCopilot growth. Designed for a founder/small team with **AI-assisted drafting** and human approval on public posts.

---

## Default week

### Monday — Growth review & planning
- Review weekly dashboard (`09-GROWTH-METRICS.md`)  
- Note wins/losses (channels, assets, CTAs)  
- Select **one hero asset** for the week (inventory promo ≥4)  
- Draft angle sheet (`04`)  
- Build outreach queue (10–20 prospects max)  
- Check GSC for coverage anomalies (don’t let growth hide site issues)

### Tuesday — Community
- Reddit: answer 3–6 questions (utility first)  
- Facebook groups: 3–6 helpful contributions (follow group rules)  
- Log URLs/UTMs only when links are appropriate  
- Capture content ideas from repeated questions → backlog  

### Wednesday — Repurposing & email
- Adapt hero asset → LinkedIn (+ optional IG carousel later)  
- Draft newsletter block (even if send is biweekly)  
- Queue founder note if relevant  
- Prepare visual: 1 chart or checklist graphic  

### Thursday — Authority outreach
- Backlink / digital PR / partner emails (batch)  
- Follow up prior week’s warm threads  
- Update prospect status in sheet  
- No mass spam; personalize first line  

### Friday — Analytics, refresh, wrap
- Update dashboard numbers  
- Follow up community threads that matter  
- Refresh notes on outdated figures if spotted  
- Write 5-bullet “next week brief”  
- Optional: light partner/LinkedIn engagement  

### Weekend (optional)
- Passive-only: nothing required  
- Light community if a hot thread needs a careful answer  

---

## Cadence variants

| Capacity | Adaptation |
|----------|------------|
| **4 hrs/week** | Mon plan + Tue community only; one outreach Thursday monthly |
| **8–10 hrs** | Full default week |
| **15+ hrs** | Add partner calls; PR batches; Tier-3 experiments |

---

## AI-assisted workflow (allowed)

| Task | AI | Human must |
|------|-----|------------|
| Angle extraction | Draft | Verify facts vs live page |
| Social captions | Draft | Tone check (`07`) |
| Outreach email | Draft | Personalize + approve |
| Newsletter block | Draft | Link + claim check |
| Community reply | Optional draft | **Always** human-send |

Never auto-post to Reddit/FB groups without review.

---

## Weekly checklist (print)

- [ ] Hero asset chosen (live URL)  
- [ ] Angles extracted  
- [ ] Community quota met  
- [ ] ≥1 LinkedIn (or scheduled)  
- [ ] Outreach touches logged  
- [ ] Dashboard updated  
- [ ] No promotion of 404/staged URLs  
- [ ] Next week brief written  

---

## Monthly add-ons (first Friday)

- Inventory rescoring for new pages  
- Partner pipeline review  
- PR mention log  
- 90-day plan phase check  

---

## Definition of a healthy week

Not “posted everywhere”—instead:

1. Real help given in communities  
2. One asset pushed with tracking  
3. Outreach moved forward  
4. Metrics recorded  
5. Site trust protected
