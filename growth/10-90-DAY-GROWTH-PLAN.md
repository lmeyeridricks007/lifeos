# 10 — 90-Day Growth Plan

**Assumption:** Technical SEO is gate-ready (~448 indexable URLs). Do **not** do everything at once.

Phases overlap slightly; each phase has a **primary focus**.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–21)

**Outcomes:** accounts checklist, metrics sheet, inventory started, newsletter plan, community SOPs ready.

| Week | Actions |
|------|---------|
| **W1** | Read growth README + strategy; create metrics spreadsheet from `09`; confirm GSC access & sitemap submitted/monitored; draft social handle checklist (do not rush posting); start asset inventory (top 20 URLs by traffic/intent) |
| **W2** | Finish inventory to ≥30 scored rows; pick 4 Tier-1 hero assets; write Reddit + FB group SOPs from brand guidelines; define lead magnet concept (checklist/PDF); map email capture points (plan only—no site build in this doc task) |
| **W3** | Set weekly cadence calendar; build outreach prospect shortlist (extend `docs/backlinks/`); package one “linkable” angle from official figures or salary/HSM; founder LinkedIn profile readiness (bio, featured link) |

**Exit criteria:** Inventory live; dashboards exist; 4 heroes chosen; SOPs written; first outreach list ready.

---

## Phase 2 — Distribution (Days 22–45)

**Outcomes:** steady community presence; first social/newsletter reps; UTMs working.

| Week | Actions |
|------|---------|
| **W4** | Launch community cadence (Tue focus): 8–12 useful replies/posts; promote only live URLs; log UTMs |
| **W5** | Repurpose Hero A across LinkedIn + one FB group thread + newsletter draft; start biweekly LinkedIn founder posts |
| **W6** | Hero B distribution; first partner intro emails (5–10); refine what angles get clicks |
| **W7** | Hero C + D; double down on winning community spaces; pause dead channels |

**Exit criteria:** ≥4 weeks of community rhythm; ≥1 asset with multi-channel pack; partner intros started.

---

## Phase 3 — Authority (Days 46–70)

**Outcomes:** PR/backlink pipeline active; data packaging shipped; mentions tracked.

| Week | Actions |
|------|---------|
| **W8** | Digital PR batch (10–15 pitches) around data/tool; track opens/replies |
| **W9** | Backlink outreach to complementary sites (resource pages, roundups); follow ups |
| **W10** | Ship/improve one citeable data page packaging (charts, methodology blurb—content ops); LinkedIn + newsletter amplify |
| **W11** | Partner resource kit v1; HR/recruiter angle posts; measure referral domains |

**Exit criteria:** Documented pitches sent; follow-up system; ≥1 authority asset promoted; partner kit exists.

---

## Phase 4 — Scale (Days 71–90)

**Outcomes:** selective Tier-3 experiments; automation assists; repeatable loops.

| Week | Actions |
|------|---------|
| **W12** | Formalize sharing loops (checklist/result CTAs); review metrics vs targets |
| **W13** | Experiment: Instagram carousel **or** Shorts—from existing hero only |
| **W14** | Retire losers; document playbook winners; plan next 90 days |

**Exit criteria:** Written “what worked”; next-quarter channel mix; inventory updated.

---

## Explicit prioritization (always on)

1. Setting up / securing social presence (checklist → later workstream)  
2. Reddit/community presence  
3. Newsletter foundation  
4. Partner pipeline  
5. Backlink/PR pipeline  
6. Linkable data assets  
7. Content repurposing  
8. Tracking  

## Explicit deferrals (until Phase 4 or later)

- Heavy YouTube long-form  
- Pinterest as primary  
- Paid social at scale  
- Daily IG aesthetic content  

---
