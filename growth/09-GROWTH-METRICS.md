# 09 — Growth Metrics

Do **not** optimize for followers alone. Optimize for compounding: links, referral, tools, email, search.

---

## North-star themes

1. **Authority:** quality referring domains / mentions  
2. **Demand:** organic + referral sessions on utility pages  
3. **Activation:** tool/checklist completions  
4. **Owned:** email subscribers and engagement  
5. **Monetization (ethical):** commercial actions with disclosure  

---

## Anti-metrics (vanity traps)

- Follower count without referral sessions  
- Post likes without clicks  
- Raw backlink count including spam  
- Impressions without landing engagement
