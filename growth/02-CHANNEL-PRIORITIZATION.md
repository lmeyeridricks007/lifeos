# 02 — Channel Prioritization

Scoring is for **ExpatCopilot as an information + tools + relocation resource**, not a lifestyle brand. Instagram/Facebook brand pages are **not** assumed primary.

**Scale:** 1 = low, 5 = high. For **Effort** and **Cost**, 5 = high (worse).  

**Composite (directionally):**  
`Impact ≈ (Traffic + SEO + Authority + Audience fit + Commercial + Scalability + Speed + Automation) − (Effort + Cost)`  
Then adjust by judgment for brand risk.

## Tier definitions

### TIER 1 — start immediately
Highest **fit × compounding SEO/referral value** for current assets.

- SEO / GSC operational rhythm  
- Reddit + niche forums  
- Facebook **groups** (not page vanity)  
- Newsletter foundation + lead magnet  
- Backlink outreach pipeline  
- Digital PR angles on data/tools  
- Package existing data as linkable assets  

### TIER 2 — build after foundation
Needs Tier-1 cadence + inventory + tracking.

- LinkedIn strategy  
- Partner / referral acquisition  
- Employer / HR kits  
- Founder-led posts  
- Productized sharing loops (checklist/result share)  

### TIER 3 — experiment
Only with a **repurposing engine** so production cost stays low.

- Instagram carousels from existing guides  
- YouTube Shorts from tool walkthroughs  
- Pinterest for visual checklists/city cards  
- Brand Facebook page as archive, not hero  
- Selective events  

### TIER 4 — optional
- Heavy paid acquisition before organic loops work  
- Low-quality directory spam  

---

## Priority order inside Tier 1 (first 30 days)

1. GSC + sitemap monitoring (already gate-ready)  
2. Asset inventory of top 30 URLs  
3. Newsletter capture plan  
4. Reddit / community SOPs  
5. Facebook group contribution SOP  
6. Backlink + PR prospect pipeline (extend `docs/backlinks/`)  
7. One linkable data packaging sprint  

---

## Channel “anti-priorities”

| Don’t over-invest early | Why |
|-------------------------|-----|
| Daily Instagram aesthetic content | High effort, weak NL-move intent vs Reddit/FB groups |
| YouTube long-form series | Production tax before message-market fit on short utility clips |
| Buying links | Brand and SEO risk |
| Mass FB page posting without groups | Low reach without ads |
