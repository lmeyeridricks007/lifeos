# 04 — Participation rules

---

## ALWAYS

- Answer the **actual** question first  
- Disclose affiliation when linking ExpatCopilot  
- Prefer official sources for binding rules  
- Admit uncertainty  
- Keep tone calm and specific  
- Log interactions in the tracker  

## NEVER

- Pretend to be an unaffiliated random user while promoting  
- Fabricate personal experience  
- Mass-drop or rotate links across threads  
- Use multiple accounts to fake support  
- Argue with moderators  
- Chase every brand mention  
- Turn every answer into a CTA  
- Give personalized legal/tax/medical conclusions  
- Scrape private groups or automate posting  

---

## ALLOWED

| Example | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Step-by-step arrival admin without any link | Pure help |
| “Roughly, net is often much lower than gross—depends on situation; verify with a calculator and Belastingdienst” | Honest + caveat |
| One deep link + “I run ExpatCopilot” after a full answer | Transparent utility |
| Linking IND/DigiD official pages | Trust |

## CAUTION

| Example | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Housing threads with scams | Easy to look promotional; lead with scam warnings |
| Tax ruling eligibility | High stakes; no guarantees |
| Posting original threads early | Promo sniff test |
| Same phrasing across 5 replies | Looks canned |

## DO NOT DO

| Example | Why |
|---------|-----|
| “Check ExpatCopilot.com” as the whole reply | Spam |
| Softening disclosure (“a site I like”) while you own it | Deceptive |
| Guaranteeing visa/ruling outcomes | Harm + liability |
| DMing strangers “I can help via my site” | Ban bait |
| Replying to every salary thread the same day with the calculator | Pattern detection |
