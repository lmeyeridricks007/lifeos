# 21 — Moderator outreach templates

Keep short. No sales pitch. Offer to follow **their** rules—including “never post links.”

---

## 1. Permission to share a resource

> Hi — I help run ExpatCopilot (free NL relocation guides/tools; not legal advice).  
> Would it be OK to share a **single deep link** when it directly answers someone’s question, with clear disclosure? Happy to stick to text-only if you prefer.

---

## 2. Offering a free checklist

> Hi — I’ve got a free arrival / document checklist members ask about often.  
> Is there a preferred way to share free resources here (wiki, weekly thread, never)? I’ll follow your format.

---

## 3. Offering a data comparison

> Hi — I published a neutral city / cost comparison (editorial, sources cited).  
> If that’s useful for the community, how would you like it shared—if at all?

---

## 4. Asking about self-promotion rules

> Hi — before I comment with any external links: what’s your current policy on sharing one’s own free tools/guides? I want to stay on the right side of the rules.

---

## 5. Proposing an AMA

> Hi — would the community be interested in an AMA on practical NL arrival admin (registration, insurance timing, common mistakes)—with clear “not legal advice” framing? I can do founder-led, not a brand ad. Happy to skip if AMAs aren’t a fit.

---

## 6. Offering to answer recurring questions

> Hi — I notice recurring questions on [topic]. I’m happy to help with **text** answers on those threads. If you’d rather I stay quiet or only reply when tagged, just say the word.

---

**Rules:** One ask · wait for reply · no follow-up spam · thank them either way · log outcome in tracker.
