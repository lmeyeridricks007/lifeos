# 18 — Weekly community cadence

Fits inside Tuesday community block from [`../11-WEEKLY-OPERATING-CADENCE.md`](../11-WEEKLY-OPERATING-CADENCE.md). Realistic for one person + AI drafts.

| Day | Focus | Time |
|-----|--------|------|
| **Mon** | Review new questions & recurring themes; score 3–5 | 20 min |
| **Tue** | Answer 2–4 high-value threads (human-approved) | 45–75 min |
| **Wed** | Log content gaps; update backlog | 20 min |
| **Thu** | One deeper contribution OR mod relationship note | 20–40 min |
| **Fri** | Metrics: referrals, removals, feedback | 15 min |
| **Weekend** | Optional lurk only | 0–20 min |

**Caps:** ≤8 substantive comments/week · ≤3 with EC links · ≤1 original post/month unless exceptional.
