# 23 — AI assistance rules

Human approval is required for **every** public interaction.

---

## AI MAY

- Categorize public questions / intents  
- Identify relevant ExpatCopilot URLs  
- Draft reply candidates (for human edit)  
- Summarize threads  
- Spot repeated questions  
- Generate content-gap reports  
- Score opportunities ([`09`](./09-OPPORTUNITY-SCORING.md))  
- Suggest official sources to cite  

---

## AI MUST NOT (autonomously)

- Post comments or create posts  
- Impersonate a person or invent “I lived in…” stories  
- Fabricate personal experience or credentials  
- Argue with users or mods  
- Bypass moderation / evade bans  
- Create accounts or join communities  
- Mass-message members  
- Hide affiliation  
- Spam or rotate links across threads  
- Scrape private/closed communities  

---

## Workflow

1. Human or AI collects **public** thread URL + question paraphrase  
2. AI drafts answer + optional link class  
3. Human edits tone, facts, disclosure  
4. Human posts manually  
5. Human logs tracker + gaps  

If unsure on risk topics ([`17`](./17-HIGH-RISK-TOPICS.md)) → answer shorter, add official source, skip EC link.
