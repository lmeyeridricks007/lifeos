# 15 — Relationship building

Goal: **trusted contributor**, not marketer.

---

## Who

| Person | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| Moderators / admins | Respect rules; ask permission; thank publicly when appropriate |
| Frequent helpful contributors | Engage their answers; don’t compete for credit |
| Expat organizers | Offer useful resources for events (no hard sell) |
| Relocation professionals | Peer exchange; co-cite carefully |
| Local experts | Quote/attribute; don’t extract free labor |

---

## Possible outcomes (earned)

Resource sharing approval · collaborative checklist · expert quote · AMA · community tool feedback · event partnership · newsletter feature · occasional backlink  

Never trade spam access (“post our link weekly”).
