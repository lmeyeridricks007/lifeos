# 01 — Community strategy

ExpatCopilot wins in communities by being the person (or quiet brand) who **reduces confusion**—not the account that drops URLs.

---

## Goals (priority order)

1. Help people genuinely  
2. Build trust and recognition  
3. Learn real questions and wording (SEO + product)  
4. Earn qualified referral traffic  
5. Surface content gaps  
6. Occasional citations / backlinks / collabs  
7. Feed tool ideas  
8. Improve intent understanding for search  
9. Early community moat (reputation), not a captive forum we own  

---

## Principles

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Answer first | Lead with brand |
| Disclose when linking | Sockpuppet or hide affiliation |
| Prefer official sources for rules | Imply we are IND/Belastingdienst |
| Deep link only when justified | Homepage spam |
| Log gaps | Chase every mention |
| Slow trust | Volume vanity |

---

## How community compounds the growth model

```
Useful answers
 → trust + mentions
 → occasional referral + tool use
 → better content from gap loop
 → stronger SEO pages
 → more people find ExpatCopilot in search
 → more community questions we can answer well
```

Facebook **groups** = distribution channel. Facebook **Page** = brand archive ([`../social-setup/`](../social-setup/)). Reddit = high-signal Q&A. Neither replaces GSC/SEO.

---

## Operating stance

- **One human-approved reply at a time**  
- AI may draft/categorize; humans post  
- Cap weekly volume ([`18-WEEKLY-CADENCE.md`](./18-WEEKLY-CADENCE.md))  
- Success includes **low removal rate**
