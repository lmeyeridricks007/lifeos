# 01 — Growth Strategy (ExpatCopilot)

**Site:** https://www.expatcopilot.com  
**Product type:** Netherlands expat information, tools, data, and relocation resource (not a consumer social app)

---

## 1. Strategic position

ExpatCopilot wins when someone mid-move asks a hard practical question and finds a clear answer, a checklist, a calculator, or a citeable number—then returns and shares.

**Core value loop:** Trustworthy utility → distribution → authority → more discovery → more utility.

Google remains the largest long-term acquisition engine. Everything else exists to (a) send referral traffic now and (b) create citations that strengthen search later.

## 3. Full compounding flow

1. **Ship or package a useful tool, dataset, or checklist** (e.g. HSM salary checker, official figures, city comparison).
2. **Distribute in communities** with context (answer first, link second).
3. **Earn citations/backlinks** from blogs, HR pages, listicles, journalists using the data/tool.
4. **Capture referral traffic** via UTMs; observe which angles convert to tool use.
5. **Strengthen domain authority and topical signals** → improved rankings for related queries.
6. **More organic users** land on guides/tools.
7. **Email capture** on high-intent moments (save checklist, salary result, moving plan).
8. **Repeat traffic + launches** via newsletter.
9. **More sharing and partner embeds** → more citations → loop continues.

**Failure mode to avoid:** Promoting thin pages or staged 404s; spamming groups; chasing followers without assets.

## 4. AARRR for ExpatCopilot

### Acquisition
- Organic search (primary long-term)
- Reddit / Facebook expat groups
- Referring domains (PR, partners, directories)
- LinkedIn (professionals, HR)
- Newsletter forwards / shares

### Activation
User does something useful in session:
- Completes a calculator / checker
- Finishes a checklist step
- Compares cities
- Saves/downloads a resource
- Reads a pillar guide past the fold / related clicks

### Retention
- Return visits for next stage of move (visa → housing → BSN → tax)
- Newsletter opens
- Bookmarks / branded search (“expatcopilot”)
- Re-use of tools when salary/city changes

### Referral
- Sharing calculator results or checklists with partner/spouse
- Community “this helped me” posts
- Employer/HR forwarding a guide
- Natural citations in blogs

### Authority
- Quality backlinks and media mentions
- Being cited for figures / methodology
- Consistent expert tone in communities
- Partner trust (relo, immigration-adjacent—without legal advice claims)

## 5. Growth principles (operating rules)

1. **One hero asset per week** — not ten half-finished campaigns.  
2. **Audience stage first** — considering vs arrived vs HSM changes the CTA.  
3. **Channel-native writing** — Reddit ≠ Instagram ≠ journalist email.  
4. **UTM everything** external; keep a simple naming convention (see metrics doc).  
5. **No fake urgency**; no pretending to be IND/Belastingdienst.  
6. **Commercial CTAs are secondary** to utility CTAs.  
7. **AI assists drafts; humans approve** community and outreach posts.

- Newsletter that can launch a tool or data update
