## How Pinterest ≠ Instagram
| Pinterest | Instagram |
|-----------|-----------|
| Searchable evergreen pins | Timely feed/social |
| Keyword titles in image + pin SEO | Hooks & carousels |
| Vertical tall pins, text-heavy OK | Cleaner visual slides |
| Save intent (planning) | Browse/engage intent |
