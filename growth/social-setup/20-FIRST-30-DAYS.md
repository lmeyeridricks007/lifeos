# 20 — First 30 days rollout

Practical schedule. **No daily posting on every platform.**

---

## Week 1 — Claim & configure

| Day | Actions |
|-----|---------|
| 1 | Business email + password vault; 2FA on Google/Meta/LinkedIn |
| 2 | Export logo mark PNGs; draft covers (Canva/Figma) |
| 3 | Create LinkedIn Company Page; complete About/logo/cover |
| 4 | Meta Business → Facebook Page + Instagram Professional |
| 5 | YouTube Brand Account + banner/icon; Pinterest Business; TikTok; X |
| 6 | All bios/links/UTMs; domain claim where available |
| 7 | Security checklist ([`09`](./09-ACCOUNT-SECURITY-GOVERNANCE.md)) + QA ([`21`](./21-SOCIAL-PROFILE-QA.md)); prepare launch assets |

**Output:** All accounts claimed; profiles complete; launch creatives in progress.

---

## Week 2 — Launch content

| Day | Actions |
|-----|---------|
| 8–9 | Finish 3 IG carousels (+ LI/FB adaptations) |
| 10 | Publish IG trio; pin posts; create Highlights shells |
| 11 | Publish LinkedIn Company trio; founder post #1 |
| 12 | Publish Facebook Page trio (or cross-post) |
| 13 | Optional: 1 YouTube Short if recorded |
| 14 | Log UTMs in metrics sheet; light engagement replies |

**Output:** Public presence with value posts—not empty profiles.

---

## Week 3 — Begin regular publishing

| Cadence | Network |
|---------|---------|
| 2× | Founder LinkedIn |
| 1–2× | Company LinkedIn |
| 2× | Instagram |
| 1× | Facebook Page |
| 0–1× | YouTube Short |
| 0 | TikTok/X unless experimenting |
| Batch | Pinterest if 9 pins ready |

Also: Reddit helpful answers (identity rules)—separate from “posting.”

---

## Week 4 — Review & adjust

- Check Insights: saves, link taps, follower quality (ignore vanity)  
- GA4 referral with social UTMs  
- Drop formats with zero utility engagement  
- Decide: activate Pinterest? keep TikTok dark?  
- Update inventory of winning pillars  
- Brief next 30 days inside weekly growth cadence  

---

## Load guardrails

| Max | Rule |
|-----|------|
| Networks actively posting | ≤4 |
| New assets designed/week | 3–5 |
| Hours social ops/week | ~3–5 inside growth budget |
