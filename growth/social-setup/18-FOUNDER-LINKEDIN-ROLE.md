# 18 — Founder LinkedIn role

Founder LinkedIn is often the **highest-ROI LinkedIn surface**. The Company Page is the brand archive and B2B credibility layer—not a substitute for a human voice.

---

## Positioning

- You are a builder of useful public resources for NL relocation—not an “expat influencer.”  
- Mix: product/learning + practical NL insights + selective personal context.  
- Do not turn the profile into 100% ExpatCopilot CTAs.

---

## Posting frequency

| Type | Cadence |
|------|---------|
| Founder posts | **2 / week** |
| Company posts | 1–2 / week |
| Comments on others’ posts | 3–5 thoughtful/week |

---

## Founder post types (good)

1. Why ExpatCopilot exists (problem → approach)  
2. Data/research insight (one chart, calm takeaway)  
3. New tool walkthrough (what it does / doesn’t)  
4. Lessons from moving to NL (specific, honest)  
5. Building public resources / editorial standards  
6. Product/AI/tech behind useful tooling (non-hype)  
7. Employer/HR angle: what candidates need before Day 1  

## When NOT to promote

- Condolence/news tragedy threads  
- Pure humblebrag without utility  
- Every comment ending in a link  
- “Like and share our page” energy  
- Claiming legal outcomes  

---

## Company vs founder cross-posting

| Pattern | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Founder original → Company reshare | Preferred for reach |
| Company original → Founder comment | Add personal note; don’t dump same text |
| Same asset same day both places | OK once/week max; vary caption |
| Identical spam across both | Avoid |

Feature ExpatCopilot on founder Featured section (NL hub or key tool)—not 12 links.

---

## Profile hygiene

- Headline: role + ExpatCopilot in plain language  
- About: problem you solve + link once  
- Experience: ExpatCopilot entry with site URL  
- Creator mode optional if it helps newsletters later
